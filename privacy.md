# Privacy notice

Source: https://meaconing.com/privacy/ · Last reviewed: 7 October 2026 · By the meaconing.com editorial team
Site: https://meaconing.com/ · Sister site: https://gnssdenial.com/

meaconing.com sets **no cookies**, runs **no analytics or advertising trackers**, has no accounts or forms, and hosts its own fonts. The only data processed is what any website must handle to deliver a page, plus any email you choose to send us.

## What this site does not do

- It does not set cookies or use `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` or similar browser storage.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, social-media or tag-manager scripts.
- It does not load fonts, scripts or images from third-party servers. Fonts are hosted on meaconing.com itself.
- It has no accounts, comment forms or newsletter sign-ups.

Because nothing is stored on or read from your device, no cookie-consent banner is needed.

## What is processed when you visit

meaconing.com is delivered through Cloudflare, Inc., which acts as our hosting and security provider. To serve a page and protect the site from abuse, Cloudflare processes technical request data such as your IP address, the page requested, the time, and your browser type. We see only aggregate statistics, such as request counts, in Cloudflare's dashboard, and we do not use them to identify individual visitors.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure, reliable website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare operates a global network, so processing can take place outside your country. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for its safeguards and retention periods.

## If you email us

Messages to contact@meaconing.com are received through Cloudflare Email Routing and forwarded to our mailbox. We use your message and email address only to reply to you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, or steps you ask us to take (Article 6(1)(f) and (b) GDPR). Please do not send sensitive personal data by email.

## Links to other sites

Pages link to sources and to related sites such as gnssdenial.com and ultrakinematic.com. Nothing is sent to them unless you click a link, and their own privacy notices then apply.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, restrict or object to its processing, and receive a copy of it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Write to contact@meaconing.com.

## Who is responsible

The controller for this site is the meaconing.com editorial team, contact@meaconing.com.
